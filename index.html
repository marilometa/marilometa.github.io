01
National nonprofit · Account Engagement & Experience Cloud

A pro bono build the client chose to continue.

Problem & scope
A national veterans nonprofit needed a complete Salesforce and Account Engagement setup for member communications. The scope covered email domain setup, subscription management, member registration, duplicate management and a review of existing dashboards.
My role
Pro bono consultant, matched through Catchafire. I designed and configured the build.
Key decisions & constraints
Members choose what they receive through an Email Preference Center, which routes them into five segmented lists. Duplicates are handled with Salesforce's native duplicate management rather than a separate tool. The work was delivered as a pro bono engagement.
What was delivered
  • Email domain authentication
  • Email Preference Center
  • Five segmented mailing lists with automatic routing
  • Experience Cloud member registration site
  • Native duplicate management
  • Audit of a 16-component dashboard
Outcome & how it was measured
After the pro bono phase, the organization secured a grant to continue the engagement.Measured by: the follow-on, grant-funded work.
02
Biotech analytics · Sales Cloud stabilization

From an at-risk go-live to executive sign-off in two weeks.

Problem & scope
A biotech analytics firm's Sales Cloud go-live was at risk. The scope was to stabilize the implementation and get it approved by executives.
My role
I took over as delivery lead and Salesforce admin.
Key decisions & constraints
The main constraint was a two-week window. I re-prioritized the backlog and ran daily stand-ups to keep the team focused on what approval required.
What was delivered
  • A prioritized backlog
  • Forecasting reports
  • Page layouts
  • Permission validation
  • User acceptance testing support
Outcome & how it was measured
Executives approved the implementation within fourteen days.Measured by: the date of executive sign-off.
03
B2B revenue operations · Lead & contact scoring

One scoring model for sales and marketing.

Problem & scope
A B2B revenue operations team needed lead and contact scoring, plus revenue reporting that sales and marketing would both rely on.
My role
I delivered the work in sprints, covering backlog grooming, sprint planning, demos and regular stakeholder reviews.
Key decisions & constraints
The data model, automation and reports were tied to business metrics and governance rules the team had defined.
What was delivered
  • Lead and contact scoring
  • Revenue analytics reports
  • Supporting data model and automation changes
Outcome
Sales and marketing work from one shared scoring model and one set of revenue reports.
